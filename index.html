FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I work on image processing, object detection, medical image analysis, real-time surveillance, quality inspection, face recognition, OCR, and custom AI/ML development.

How long does a typical project take?

Simple projects take 1-2 weeks, while full AI systems with deployment can take 2-6 months. I provide detailed timelines during the proposal phase.

Do you provide the source code?

Yes! All projects include complete source code with documentation. You own 100% of the code and models after final payment.

Can you deploy models to production?

Absolutely! I deploy to cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure), edge devices (Jetson, Raspberry Pi), mobile apps, and web applications.

What is your pricing model?

I offer fixed-price projects, hourly rates, and monthly retainers. Initial consultation is always free.

Do you offer post-project support?

Yes! Every project includes 30 days of free support. Extended support plans are available on retainer basis.
